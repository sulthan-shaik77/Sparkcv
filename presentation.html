IDEA TITLE & OVERVIEW
Proposed Solution (Idea / Prototype Overview)
2
⚠️ THE INDUSTRY CHALLENGE
  • 75%+ Automated ATS Rejection: Over three-quarters of candidate resumes are filtered out algorithmically before ever reaching human recruiters.
  • Blind Keyword Cramming: Applicants lack visibility into the exact technical ontology of target roles, resorting to ineffective keyword stuffing.
  • Lack of Actionable Bridge: Conventional scanners provide superficial percentage scores but fail to tell candidates what concrete projects to build or how to rewrite bullets to bridge gaps.
  • Expensive & Slow LLMs: Existing solutions rely on recurring, slow, privacy-risky external cloud APIs (OpenAI/Anthropic) that leak PII.
✨ THE SPARKCV SOLUTION
  • Multi-Format Ingestion: Zero-dependency parser for PDF, DOCX, and TXT with native in-memory XML/ZIP stream extraction.
  • 250+ Hard-Skill Ontology: Domain-specific taxonomy covering Languages, Frameworks, Cloud, Databases, and ML with strict boundary regex.
  • Balanced Hybrid Scoring: 50% Skill Coverage Ratio + 50% TF-IDF Cosine Similarity for fair, deterministic evaluation.
  • Dynamic Portfolio Projects: Generates 2-3 production-grade project blueprints (tech stack + metrics) to fill missing skills.
  • Google XYZ Bullet Synthesizer: Transforms weak statements into quantified achievements: Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z].
  • ATS Structural Linter: Audits metric density, active verb power, and formatting traps with instant markdown audit export.

🔬 MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION & SCORING EQUATION

Balanced Hybrid Formula:

Final Score = (0.50 × Skill Coverage Ratio) + (0.50 × TF-IDF Cosine Similarity)

Where Skill Coverage Ratio = Matched Skills / Total Required Skills, and TF-IDF Cosine Similarity assesses contextual alignment across all unigram and bigram vectors.

Verdict Tiers:

❌ < 50%: Needs Major Improvement

⚠️ 50% - 75%: Moderate Alignment

✅ > 75%: Strong Match (Application Ready)

RISK MANAGEMENT & VIABILITY
Feasibility, Viability & Risk Mitigation
5
⚡ RISK: Non-Standard File Formats
Complex tables, multi-column layouts, and non-UTF-8 encodings failing to extract text.
🛡️ MITIGATION: Multi-Parser Fallback
Triple-tier decoding (UTF-8, Latin-1, CP1252) plus raw in-memory XML traversal for corrupt DOCX.
⚡ RISK: Keyword Stuffing Vulnerability
Candidates pasting raw keyword dumps to artificially inflate scores without real experience.
🛡️ MITIGATION: Dual-Metric Vector Blend
50% TF-IDF Cosine Similarity verifies contextual phrasing, n-grams, and semantic sentence structure.
⚡ RISK: Short Skill Name Collisions
Languages like 'Go', 'R', or 'C' causing false positives by matching common conversational words.
🛡️ MITIGATION: Strict Boundary Regex
Protected word boundaries (\bGo\b, \bC\+\+\b, \b\.NET\b) with technical context filters.
💡 COMMERCIAL VIABILITY & ENTERPRISE SCALING
100% offline-capable mathematical NLP ensures zero marginal cost per scan. Ready for enterprise licensing across University Placement Portals, EdTech Bootcamps, and Corporate HR pipelines with zero privacy risk.
