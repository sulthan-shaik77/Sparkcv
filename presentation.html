SCORING METHODOLOGY
How the Balanced Match Score Works
Department of Computer Science and Engineering • Summer Internship-1, Review 1
7
⚖️ Dual Mathematical Balance

Final Score = (50% Skill Match) + (50% Semantic Meaning Match)

• Skill Match (50%): Do you have the specific technical tools the job asks for?
• Meaning Match (50%): Does your experience and wording actually fit the job description?

Using both parts stops people from just stuffing keywords to cheat the system!

🎯 3-Tier Calibrated Verdicts
  • Below 50%: ❌ Weak — Missing critical core skills; needs major rework.
  • 50% – 75%: ⚠️ Moderate — Solid foundation, but needs tweaking.
  • Above 75%: ✅ Strong Match — Perfect alignment; ready to apply!
